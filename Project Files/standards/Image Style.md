# RecipeKit Image Style

**Preset family:** Editorial Minimal  
**Master aspect ratio:** 4:3 (landscape)  
**Status:** User-approved direction; preset names remain adjustable.

## Shared photographic identity

Create convincing premium-but-approachable cookbook photography. Every image should plausibly come from **one photographer with one lighting and editing approach**, even if the surface or dish changes. Use soft directional daylight, restrained shadows, warm-neutral white balance, true-to-food colors, a subtle texture, realistic depth of field, and uncluttered compositions.

- Framing: 4:3 landscape master; default to 3/4 view, use overhead when it best explains the food.
- Subjects: prepared final dish, accurate ingredients and plausible preparation/texture.
- Props: minimal, credible, food-relevant, never unlisted garnishes, sauces, elaborate sides or branded goods.
- Surface and plate: a small curated palette; neutral linen, light stone, warm wood or simple ceramics.
- Serving vessel variety: do not assume every casserole should be photographed in a traditional rectangular casserole or baking dish. Use appropriate round or oval bakeware, skillets, shallow bowls, plates, or a realistic served portion when it presents the finished recipe well. Vary vessels across the collection; remain faithful to the food's baked texture and any equipment constraints in the source.
- Text: none. No labels, logo, watermark or fake restaurant branding.
- Lighting/finish: steady across presets; changing a scene must not make it look like a different cookbook.
- Disclosure: mark asset origin as AI-generated. Do not call the result a genuine photograph of food the user cooked.

All presets share the **same camera realism, exposure, restrained color grading, lighting softness, and editing treatment**.

## Prompt template

> Create a photorealistic, premium editorial cookbook image of the **finished dish** described by the accompanying recipe. Use only ingredients, toppings and textures plausibly supported by its ingredients and cooking method. Compose as a 4:3 landscape image with soft directional natural light, balanced warm-neutral color, restrained saturation, lifelike ceramics and surfaces, authentic food detail, and a shallow but credible depth of field. Choose the `[SCENE PRESET]` environment while preserving the collection’s consistent photographic identity. Avoid text, branding, watermarks, extra garnishes, sauces and side dishes. Favor an appetizing but believable presentation.

## Review checklist

- Visually plausible for ingredients and steps? Not accidentally adding ingredients, sauce or garnish?
- Correct dish form, portion, texture, and cookware/plating cues? Are casserole images being varied rather than defaulting to rectangular baking dishes?
- Matches the shared editorial look and 4:3 master ratio?
- Attractive natural lighting and a quiet, appropriate scene?
- Clearly labelled as AI-generated in recipe asset metadata?
- Approved by the collection owner?

Generated assets may require subjective review; never auto-approve simply because generation succeeded. The first approved image used `natural-linen`, with a cut ham-and-egg burrito on neutral stoneware.
